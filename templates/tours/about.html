Our story

Local, safe and fair – since day one

We are a locally owned travel company based in Moshi & Arusha, founded by {{ FOUNDER_NAME }}. We specialize in authentic, safe and affordable adventures across Tanzania: climbing Kilimanjaro and Mount Meru, safaris through the Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire and Lake Manyara, and cultural journeys in between.

Every guide, cook and porter on our team is local. That means people who know these mountains and parks like home – because they are home – and money that stays in the communities around them.

We keep our groups well looked after: emergency oxygen, a first aid kit and a pulse oximeter on every climb, licensed guides, clear prices with park fees included, and fair pay for our crew. And for every climber, we plant a tree.

— {{ FOUNDER_NAME }}, founder
